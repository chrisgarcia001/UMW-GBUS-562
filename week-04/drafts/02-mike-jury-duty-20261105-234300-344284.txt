Good news — you won't need to use your vacation days for jury duty. Per our Vacation and Time Off Policy, Hartwell pays your regular wages for up to 10 business days of jury duty per year, and it does not reduce your vacation or sick balance (leave-policy.html).

What to do:

If your jury service ends up lasting longer than 10 business days, reach out to HR to discuss next steps.

Let us know if you have any other questions!
