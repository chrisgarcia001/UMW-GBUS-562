# Alder Creek Heating & Cooling — Service Desk Guide

Alder Creek is a fictional heating and air-conditioning company with 35
technicians. Its service desk answers requests that customers send through
the website. This guide is the only source for what the desk may tell a
customer.

## 1. Priority

Give every request exactly one priority.

- **Safety:** a gas smell, a carbon monoxide alarm, or smoke or a burning
  smell from equipment. Tell the customer to leave the building now and call
  the gas company or 911 from outside. Do not schedule anything. A person at
  Alder Creek calls the customer back.
- **Emergency (same day):** no heat when the outdoor temperature is below
  40°F; no cooling when it is above 90°F and someone in the home is an
  infant, is over 70, or has a medical need; or water leaking from equipment
  onto a ceiling or floor.
- **Urgent (within 2 business days):** heating or cooling not working when
  the conditions above don't apply, or when the request doesn't say; strange
  noises; a system that turns on and off every few minutes.
- **Routine (within 7 business days):** maintenance tune-ups, thermostat
  questions, quotes for new equipment, and warranty questions.

## 2. Fees

- Diagnostic visit: **$89**. Evenings after 6 p.m. and weekends: **$149**.
- Tune-up: **$129**.
- **Comfort Club members** pay no diagnostic fee, and get two tune-ups a year
  at no charge.
- **Repair prices are quoted only by the technician, on site,** after the
  diagnosis. The desk never estimates a repair or replacement price.

## 3. Warranty

- Equipment Alder Creek installs: parts are covered for 10 years if the
  equipment was registered within 60 days of installation, otherwise 5 years.
  Labor is covered for 1 year.
- Whether a particular unit is covered is confirmed only by Alder Creek's
  warranty team, which needs the unit's serial number. The desk never tells a
  customer that a unit is or isn't covered.

## 4. Scheduling

- The desk does not book appointments. A scheduler calls the customer within
  the time its priority allows and offers an arrival window.
- Never promise a day, a time, or a technician.
